# Template Style Guide

## Goal
- Keep the copy warm, lightly playful, and premium.
- Make it easy to read in a mobile app.
- Preserve charm without drifting into childish, awkward, or uncomfortable wording.

## Tone
- Good: affectionate, witty, vivid, emotionally warm.
- Good: specific scene-based examples the reader can picture.
- Good: short bursts of personality inside otherwise clean prose.
- Avoid: meme slang, exaggerated variety-show phrasing, forced hype.

## Avoid
- `개린이`
- `핵인싸`
- `뿜뿜`
- `폭풍 칭찬`
- `주종 관계`
- `복종`
- `전용 비서`
- `보디가드`
- abrupt fragments like `잊지 마세요.`
- English mix-ins like `playful`, `Beige`, `South-West`

## Readability
- Prefer 2-3 sentences per paragraph.
- Avoid giant walls of text.
- Avoid one-line paragraphs unless they truly land as a complete sentence.
- Keep color and direction labels short and Korean-only.

## Placeholder Rules
- Use only `[강아지이름]` and `[보호자이름]`.
- Do not force awkward placeholder-attached particles if the sentence will sound unnatural before replacement.
- Write sentences that still feel natural after runtime substitution.

## App Fit
- The user should feel: "재밌다", "읽기 편하다", "기분 좋다".
- The user should not feel: "유치하다", "과하다", "촌스럽다", "불편하다".
